📌 Introduction & Problem Statement

🎯 Problem Statement

  • Traditional crowdfunding platforms charge high fees (5-10%)
  • Lack of transparency in fund utilization
  • Centralized control over funds
  • Geographical restrictions on donations
  • Risk of fraud and misuse of funds

💡 Our Solution

  • Decentralized platform with no intermediaries
  • Complete transparency via blockchain
  • Smart contracts ensure trustless transactions
  • Global access with cryptocurrency
  • Automated refunds if goals aren't met
